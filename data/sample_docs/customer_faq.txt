================================================================================
PLATEWISE — TOP CUSTOMER FAQs
Last Updated: 10 February 2025
================================================================================

Q1. How can I track my order?
A1. Once the restaurant accepts your order, you can track it in real-time under 
    the "My Orders" section. The map will activate as soon as the delivery partner 
    picks up the food.

Q2. What if my food arrives cold or damaged?
A2. We apologize if this happens. Please go to My Orders > Select the Order > 
    Report an Issue > "Food Quality/Damage" within 2 hours of delivery. Upload 
    clear photos of the damaged items, and we will initiate a refund or 
    replacement based on our Refund Policy.

Q3. Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
A3. You can change your address only if the restaurant has not yet handed the 
    food to the delivery partner AND the new address is within 1 km of the 
    original address. If it's further, the order will have to be cancelled.

Q4. Why did my order get cancelled automatically?
A4. Orders are auto-cancelled if the restaurant fails to accept it within 15 
    minutes, or if no delivery partners are available in your area due to extreme 
    weather or high demand. A full refund is processed instantly.

Q5. How do I apply a promo code?
A5. On the checkout page, click on "Apply Coupon" below the bill summary. 
    Enter your code or select an available offer.

Q6. What is PlateWise Pro?
A6. PlateWise Pro is our premium membership that offers free delivery on orders 
    above ₹199, priority customer support, and an "On-Time or Free" guarantee.
