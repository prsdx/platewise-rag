================================================================================
PLATEWISE — DELIVERY SLA & COMPENSATION POLICY
Document Version: 3.0 | Effective Date: 1 January 2025
================================================================================

1. ESTIMATED TIME OF ARRIVAL (ETA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The ETA displayed at checkout is an algorithmic estimation based on restaurant 
prep time, traffic conditions, and delivery partner availability. It is not a 
guaranteed delivery time, except for orders placed under the "PlateWise Pro" 
subscription.

2. LATE DELIVERY COMPENSATION (STANDARD ORDERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If an order arrives more than 20 minutes past the original ETA:
- The customer is eligible for a standard ₹50 "Late Apology Credit".
- To claim, the customer must tap "Order was late" in the Help section within 
  12 hours of delivery.
- Note: This does not apply during extreme weather conditions, natural disasters,
  or city-wide bandhs/strikes.

3. PLATEWISE PRO: "ON-TIME OR FREE" GUARANTEE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For PlateWise Pro subscribers, eligible orders carry an "On-Time or Free" badge.
- If the order arrives even 1 minute past the promised ETA, the customer receives
  a 100% refund of the food value (up to ₹500) as PlateWise Credits.
- The refund is processed automatically within 24 hours.
- Taxes and packaging charges are not refunded.

4. EXTREME DELAYS & FOOD SAFETY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
If an order is delayed by more than 60 minutes past the ETA:
- The customer has the right to refuse delivery at the doorstep.
- A full refund (including all fees and taxes) will be issued to the original 
  payment method.
- PlateWise strictly instructs delivery partners not to deliver food that has 
  been in transit for more than 90 minutes due to food safety and temperature 
  control regulations.

5. DELIVERY PARTNER WAIT TIME
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Upon reaching the customer's location, the delivery partner will wait for a 
maximum of 10 minutes. 
- The partner will attempt to call the customer at least 3 times.
- PlateWise Support will send an app notification and SMS.
- If the customer remains unreachable after 10 minutes, the order will be 
  marked "Customer Unavailable" and cancelled. No refund will be provided.

6. ADDRESS CHANGES POST-DISPATCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Once the delivery partner has picked up the order, the delivery address cannot 
be changed if the new location is more than 1 kilometer away from the original. 
If the new address is within 1 km, a ₹30 redirection fee applies.
