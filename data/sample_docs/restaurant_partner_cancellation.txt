================================================================================
PLATEWISE — PARTNER RESTAURANT CANCELLATION & PENALTY POLICY
Document Version: 1.4 | Effective Date: 1 March 2025
================================================================================

This document outlines the policies regarding order cancellations initiated or 
caused by Restaurant Partners on the PlateWise platform.

================================================================================
1. MERCHANT-INITIATED CANCELLATIONS (MIC)
================================================================================

A Merchant-Initiated Cancellation occurs when a restaurant cancels an order after
initially accepting it, or fails to accept an order within the 15-minute SLA.

1.1 Acceptable Cancellation Reasons (No Penalty):
    - Item out of stock (Must update inventory on the dashboard within 5 mins).
    - Emergency kitchen closure (fire, power outage, equipment breakdown).
    - Delivery partner unassigned for >30 minutes.

1.2 Unacceptable Cancellation Reasons (Penalty Applies):
    - "Kitchen too busy" (Restaurants must use the 'Busy Mode' toggle instead).
    - Price discrepancy (Restaurant failed to update prices on the portal).
    - Closing time approached (If order was placed before closing time).

================================================================================
2. PENALTY STRUCTURE
================================================================================

If an order is cancelled due to an unacceptable reason, the following penalties apply:

2.1 Cancellation Penalty Fee
    - Tier 1 (1st to 3rd cancellation in a week): ₹50 per order.
    - Tier 2 (4th to 10th cancellation in a week): ₹100 per order.
    - Tier 3 (11+ cancellations in a week): ₹200 per order + temporary suspension.

2.2 Visibility Downgrade
    - Restaurants exceeding a 3% cancellation rate over a 7-day rolling period
      will be deprioritized in the PlateWise search algorithm and removed from
      the "Recommended" carousel for 48 hours.

2.3 Customer Compensation Charge
    - If a cancellation occurs more than 20 minutes after order acceptance, the 
      restaurant will be charged an additional ₹50 customer inconvenience fee, 
      which is passed directly to the customer as PlateWise Credit.

================================================================================
3. AUTO-CANCELLATION BY SYSTEM
================================================================================

3.1 No Acceptance
    If an order is not accepted on the merchant terminal within 15 minutes, 
    the system auto-cancels the order. This counts as a Merchant Cancellation 
    and incurs a ₹50 penalty.

3.2 Prep Time Violation
    If an order is not marked "Food Ready" within 45 minutes of the expected 
    prep time, and the customer requests cancellation, the order will be 
    cancelled at the merchant's expense (Full order value deduction).

================================================================================
4. DISPUTES & WAIVERS
================================================================================

Restaurants may dispute a cancellation penalty within 72 hours via the 
Partner Dashboard -> Help Center -> "Dispute Penalty". 

Valid proof must be provided (e.g., CCTV footage showing power outage, POS logs).
Our Partner Support team resolves disputes within 3-5 business days.
