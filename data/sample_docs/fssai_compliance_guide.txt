================================================================================
PLATEWISE — FSSAI COMPLIANCE & HYGIENE GUIDE FOR PARTNERS
Reference Guide for Food Safety and Standards Authority of India Regulations
================================================================================

1. FSSAI REGISTRATION & LICENSING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every food business operator (FBO) partnering with PlateWise MUST possess a 
valid FSSAI License or Registration Certificate.

- Turnover < ₹12 Lakhs: FSSAI Registration Certificate (Form A).
- Turnover ₹12 Lakhs - ₹20 Crores: State FSSAI License (Form B).
- Turnover > ₹20 Crores / Operating in multiple states: Central FSSAI License.

The 14-digit FSSAI number must be displayed prominently on the restaurant 
premises and printed on all customer invoices. PlateWise requires the FSSAI 
certificate to be uploaded to the Partner Dashboard. Expired certificates will 
result in automatic delisting until renewed.

2. PREMISES & FACILITY HYGIENE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Floors, walls, and ceilings must be clean, intact, and easy to sanitize.
- Adequate ventilation must be provided to prevent condensation and grease buildup.
- Pest control measures must be documented and conducted monthly by a licensed agency.
- Pets and stray animals are strictly prohibited inside the kitchen/prep areas.
- Potable water (drinking quality) must be used for all cooking and ice-making.
  Water testing reports must be available upon request.

3. PERSONAL HYGIENE OF FOOD HANDLERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Food handlers must wear clean protective clothing, hairnets, and gloves.
- Regular handwashing with soap and warm water is mandatory.
- Personnel suffering from infectious diseases (fever, cold, cuts, skin infections)
  must not handle food or enter the food prep area.
- Annual medical fitness certificates (including tests for typhoid and tuberculosis) 
  are mandatory for all kitchen staff.
- No smoking, spitting, or chewing tobacco/gum in food handling areas.

4. FOOD STORAGE & TEMPERATURE CONTROL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Raw and cooked food must be stored separately to prevent cross-contamination.
- Vegetarian and Non-Vegetarian items must have separate storage and prep areas.
- Cold storage requirements:
    - Refrigerators: Below 5°C.
    - Freezers: Below -18°C.
- Hot holding: Cooked food meant for hot serving must be kept at > 60°C.
- First-In-First-Out (FIFO) methodology must be strictly followed for raw materials.

5. PACKAGING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Food-grade packaging material MUST be used.
- Single-use plastics banned by local municipalities are strictly prohibited.
- Hot liquids (soups, gravies) must be packed in heat-resistant, leak-proof containers.
- All delivery parcels must be sealed with a tamper-evident PlateWise tape or sticker.

6. AUDITS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PlateWise conducts surprise hygiene audits via third-party agencies (e.g., Equinox Labs).
Restaurants failing an audit (scoring below 60%) will be suspended from the 
platform until a corrective action plan is implemented and a re-audit is passed.
