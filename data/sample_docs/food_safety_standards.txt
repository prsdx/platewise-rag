================================================================================
PLATEWISE — FOOD SAFETY STANDARDS REFERENCE
Document Version: 2.1 | Last Updated: January 2025
================================================================================

This document serves as a quick reference for PlateWise staff regarding basic 
food safety standards. 

1. THE DANGER ZONE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bacteria multiply most rapidly between 5°C and 60°C (41°F and 140°F). 
This range is known as the "Danger Zone."
- Cold food must be kept below 5°C.
- Hot food must be kept above 60°C.
- Food should not be left in the Danger Zone for more than 2 hours.

2. COOKING TEMPERATURES (MINIMUM SAFE INTERNAL TEMPERATURES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Poultry (Chicken, Duck, Turkey): 74°C (165°F)
- Minced/Ground Meats (Beef, Pork, Lamb): 71°C (160°F)
- Whole Cuts of Meat (Beef, Pork, Lamb): 63°C (145°F) + 3 min rest
- Fish and Seafood: 63°C (145°F)
- Leftovers/Reheating: 74°C (165°F)

3. CROSS-CONTAMINATION PREVENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Use separate cutting boards and utensils for raw meat and ready-to-eat foods.
- Store raw meat below cooked and ready-to-eat foods in the refrigerator to 
  prevent juices from dripping.
- Wash hands thoroughly after handling raw meat.

4. THAWING SAFE PRACTICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Never thaw food at room temperature. Safe methods include:
- In the refrigerator (safest method).
- Under cold running water.
- In the microwave (must be cooked immediately after).
