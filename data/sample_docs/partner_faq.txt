================================================================================
PLATEWISE — PARTNER RESTAURANT FAQs
Last Updated: 10 February 2025
================================================================================

Q1. How do I temporarily stop receiving orders?
A1. If your kitchen is overwhelmed, use the "Busy Mode" toggle on your Partner 
    Dashboard. You can pause orders for 30 minutes, 1 hour, or until the next day. 
    Do NOT accept orders and then cancel them, as this incurs a penalty.

Q2. When are payouts processed?
A2. Payouts are processed on a weekly cycle. Earnings from Monday to Sunday are 
    transferred to your registered bank account by the following Wednesday.

Q3. How do I update my menu prices?
A3. You can edit item prices directly from the "Menu Management" tab on the 
    Dashboard. Price updates take up to 2 hours to reflect on the customer app.

Q4. What do I do if the assigned delivery partner doesn't show up?
A4. If the partner hasn't arrived 15 minutes after the food is marked "Ready", 
    click "Report Partner Delay" on the active order screen. The system will 
    automatically reassign a new partner.

Q5. A customer called asking to customize their order. What should I do?
A5. Advise the customer that customizations can only be guaranteed if they were 
    added in the "Order Notes" during checkout. You may accommodate minor requests 
    at your discretion, but PlateWise will not cover any additional costs incurred.

Q6. How can I run a discount campaign?
A6. Navigate to the "Promotions" tab on your Dashboard. You can set up percentage 
    discounts (e.g., 20% off) or flat discounts (e.g., ₹50 off on ₹300) and choose 
    the campaign duration.
