================================================================================
PLATEWISE — CUSTOMER REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY
Document Version: 2.1 | Effective Date: 1 January 2025
================================================================================

This policy governs all refund and cancellation requests made by customers on the
PlateWise platform. By placing an order, you agree to the terms below.

================================================================================
1. ORDER CANCELLATION
================================================================================

1.1  FREE CANCELLATION WINDOW
     You may cancel your order without any charge within 3 minutes of placing it,
     provided the restaurant has not yet confirmed the order.

1.2  CANCELLATION AFTER RESTAURANT CONFIRMATION
     Once the restaurant confirms the order, cancellation is subject to the
     restaurant's individual policy. A cancellation fee of up to 50% of the
     order value may be charged.

1.3  CANCELLATION AFTER DISPATCH
     Orders cannot be cancelled after the delivery partner has picked up the food.
     If you no longer want the order, you may reject it at the door, but no refund
     will be issued for a rejection at the door unless the food is damaged or
     incorrect.

1.4  HOW TO CANCEL
     To cancel, go to: My Orders → Select Order → Cancel Order.
     Cancellations via customer support may take longer to process.

================================================================================
2. REFUND ELIGIBILITY
================================================================================

You are eligible for a full refund in the following situations:

2.1  LATE DELIVERY REFUND
     If your order arrives more than 30 minutes after the estimated delivery time
     (ETA) displayed at checkout, you are eligible for a ₹50 platform credit or
     a 10% refund of the order value, whichever is higher. To claim: raise a
     "Late Delivery" ticket within 24 hours of delivery.

2.2  WRONG ITEMS DELIVERED
     If you receive an item different from what you ordered (wrong dish, wrong
     size, wrong variant), you are eligible for a full refund or re-delivery of
     the correct item. Photo evidence required.

2.3  MISSING ITEMS
     If items are missing from your order, you are eligible for a refund of the
     exact value of the missing items. Raise a "Missing Item" request within 2
     hours of delivery. Photo of the delivered items required.

2.4  FOOD QUALITY ISSUES
     If the food is spoiled, undercooked, or contains a foreign object, you are
     eligible for a full refund. You must raise a complaint within 2 hours of
     delivery with photo or video evidence. PlateWise reserves the right to
     investigate and may offer a partial refund or platform credit based on the
     findings.

2.5  RESTAURANT CLOSED / ORDER NOT ACCEPTED
     If the restaurant does not accept your order within 15 minutes, the order is
     automatically cancelled and a full refund is processed.

2.6  DUPLICATE CHARGE
     If your account has been charged more than once for the same order, a full
     refund of the duplicate charge is processed within 2 business days.

================================================================================
3. NON-REFUNDABLE SITUATIONS
================================================================================

The following are NOT eligible for refunds:

- Change of mind after the cancellation window has elapsed.
- Incorrect delivery address provided by the customer.
- Customer not available to receive the order (after 2 delivery attempts).
- Subjective quality complaints ("food was too spicy" / "portion felt small")
  without objective evidence of a defect.
- Orders paid using expired or invalid promo codes.
- Platform credits/coupons are non-refundable and cannot be converted to cash.

================================================================================
4. REFUND TIMELINES
================================================================================

  Original Payment Method    | Refund Timeline
  ---------------------------|-----------------
  UPI / IMPS                 | 1–3 business days
  Debit Card                 | 3–5 business days
  Credit Card                | 5–7 business days
  Net Banking                | 3–5 business days
  PlateWise Wallet           | Instant (within 30 minutes)
  Platform Credit            | Instant (within 30 minutes)

Delays beyond the above timelines: contact your bank. PlateWise support can
provide a refund reference number for your bank to trace the transaction.

================================================================================
5. HOW TO RAISE A REFUND REQUEST
================================================================================

Step 1: Open the PlateWise app or website.
Step 2: Go to My Orders → Select the affected order.
Step 3: Tap "Report a Problem" and select the relevant issue category.
Step 4: Upload photos/videos if prompted.
Step 5: Submit the request.

Our support team will respond within 24 hours. Complex cases (quality disputes,
partial refunds) may take up to 5 business days to resolve.

For urgent issues: Live chat is available 8:00 AM – 11:00 PM IST, 7 days a week.
Phone support: 1800-XXX-XXXX (toll-free), 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM IST.

================================================================================
6. APPEALS
================================================================================

If you disagree with a refund decision, you may appeal within 7 days by emailing
support@platewise.com with the order ID and reason for appeal. Appeals are
reviewed by the escalation team within 5 business days. The decision of the
escalation team is final.

7. POLICY CHANGES
================================================================================

PlateWise reserves the right to update this policy at any time. The latest version
will always be available at platewise.com/policies/refund. Continued use of the
platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
