================================================================================
PLATEWISE — COMMON ALLERGEN GUIDE
For Restaurant Partners & Support Staff
================================================================================

Restaurants must accurately declare the presence of any of the following 14 
major food allergens in their dishes on the PlateWise menu platform.

THE MAJOR 14 ALLERGENS:
1.  Peanuts
2.  Tree Nuts (Almonds, walnuts, cashews, pistachios, macadamia, etc.)
3.  Milk / Dairy (Cheese, butter, cream, yogurt, whey)
4.  Eggs
5.  Fish
6.  Crustacean Shellfish (Crab, lobster, shrimp)
7.  Molluscs (Oysters, squid, scallops)
8.  Soy (Soybeans, soy sauce, tofu)
9.  Wheat / Gluten (Rye, barley, oats)
10. Sesame (Seeds, oil, tahini)
11. Mustard (Liquid, powder, seeds)
12. Celery (Stalks, leaves, seeds, celeriac)
13. Lupin (Flour, seeds)
14. Sulphites (Preservatives often found in dried fruits, wine > 10mg/kg)

MENU LABELING REQUIREMENTS:
- If a dish contains an allergen as a direct ingredient, it MUST be tagged.
- If a dish is prepared in a shared fryer or on a shared grill with allergens, 
  a "May Contain" or cross-contamination warning must be displayed.
- For severe customer allergies noted in the "Order Instructions", restaurants 
  must sanitize prep areas and use clean utensils to prepare the specific dish.
