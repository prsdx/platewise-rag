================================================================================
PLATEWISE — SURGE PRICING & PEAK HOUR OPERATIONS POLICY
Internal & Partner Operations Guide
================================================================================

1. WHAT CONSTITUTES "PEAK HOURS"?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Peak hours are dynamically triggered by the PlateWise algorithm when:
- Order volume in a specific 3km radius exceeds active delivery partner capacity 
  by a ratio of 3:1.
- Severe weather conditions (heavy rain, waterlogging) are reported.
- Major public holidays or local festivals (e.g., Diwali, New Year's Eve, match days).

Standard historical peak hours:
- Lunch: 12:30 PM - 2:30 PM
- Dinner: 7:30 PM - 10:00 PM
- Weekends (Fri-Sun) experience 40% higher volume.

2. DYNAMIC SURGE PRICING (DELIVERY FEE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
During peak hours, a "Surge Fee" is applied to the customer's delivery charge.
- Tier 1 Surge: +₹20 (High Demand)
- Tier 2 Surge: +₹40 (Very High Demand / Light Rain)
- Tier 3 Surge: +₹60 (Severe Conditions / Extreme Shortage)

This extra fee goes directly to the delivery partner as a "Peak Bonus" to 
incentivize them to log in and accept orders during difficult conditions.
PlateWise Pro members are exempt from Tier 1 & 2 surge fees, but pay a reduced 
fee during Tier 3.

3. RESTAURANT OPERATIONS DURING PEAK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Preparation SLAs: Automatically extended by +10 minutes in the system.
- Promotions: Discounts and "Buy 1 Get 1" offers can be dynamically paused 
  by restaurants if they opt into "Peak Auto-Pause".
- Order Limiting: If a restaurant fails to accept 3 consecutive orders within 
  the SLA during a surge, the system will automatically place the restaurant 
  on "Busy Mode" for 30 minutes to clear the backlog.
