================================================================================
PLATEWISE — VEGAN & VEGETARIAN CERTIFICATION STANDARDS
================================================================================

To list items under the "Pure Veg" or "Vegan" categories on PlateWise, 
restaurants must adhere strictly to these guidelines.

1. "PURE VEG" RESTAURANT BADGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The entire kitchen facility must be 100% free of meat, fish, poultry, and eggs.
- No animal products (other than dairy and honey) can be present on the premises.
- Delivery packages must use the Green Dot symbol.

2. VEGETARIAN ITEM TAG [V]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dish does not contain meat, poultry, fish, or seafood.
- Dish does not contain animal-derived rennet (commonly used in some cheeses).
- Dish does not contain gelatin (derived from animal collagen).
- Dish does not contain cochineal/carmine (red food dye derived from insects).
- Dairy and eggs are permitted in general vegetarian items (Note: In India, 
  egg is typically classified as Non-Veg, but for global standards, lacto-ovo 
  vegetarian is standard. PlateWise India uses the strict lacto-vegetarian 
  definition for [V], where egg is excluded).

3. VEGAN ITEM TAG [VG]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Dish contains NO animal products whatsoever.
- Excludes: Meat, fish, poultry, eggs, dairy (milk, butter, cheese, ghee, cream),
  honey, gelatin, and rennet.
- Cross-contamination: Must be prepared using separate utensils and cookware 
  from non-vegan items. If a shared fryer is used, the dish cannot be tagged 
  as Vegan.

4. JAIN FOOD STANDARDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Must follow all "Pure Veg" rules.
- Must strictly exclude all root vegetables (onions, garlic, potatoes, carrots, 
  radishes, turnips, beetroot).
- Separate prep areas are highly recommended.
